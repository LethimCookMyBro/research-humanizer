# Version 2.0 change notes

Prepared on 12 November 2026 from the supplied research-paper-craft v1.1 package.

## What changed

The primary file now contains a concise operating contract and direct routes to eight topics. It no longer requires the whole guide at the beginning of a full-paper task. The original Thai and English R00 through R30 rule bodies are preserved without substantive changes. They are reorganized by task rather than duplicated in a single always-loaded file. R31 is retained as historical attribution in origin-v1.1.md.

The text checker, its 18 unit tests, and the two working templates are unchanged. New static checks cover routing, reference availability, language alignment, core controls, and package structure. Four routing scenarios supplement the 36 existing model-evaluation scenarios. Scenario definitions are not executed behavioral results.

The historical policy-source notes were not reverified as current venue policies. They remain in sources-v1.1.md with their original date. Actual paper preparation must check the target venue's current requirements.
