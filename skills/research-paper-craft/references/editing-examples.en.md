# Source-bounded editing examples

All notes are synthetic teaching fixtures. These are not real research results, researcher-approved revisions or recorded model responses. Several rewrites may be valid. Do not import a fixture detail into a real manuscript.

## W01

Source note

The implementation combines sensor readings. No evaluation results exist in the note.

Draft

> Our groundbreaking framework seamlessly integrates sensor measurements and demonstrates robust real-world performance.

One acceptable response

> The method combines sensor readings.

Preserve

Do not assert robustness or evaluation. Do not invent sensors or processing steps.

## W03

Source note

The logger records each action and the corresponding workpiece state.

Draft

> The logger records each action and the corresponding workpiece state.

One acceptable response

> The logger records each action and the corresponding workpiece state.

Preserve

Do not replace logger or workpiece state with invented names. Unchanged is acceptable.

## W05

Source note

Only successful trials were included in mean completion time. Failed trials were excluded.

Draft

> Only successful trials were included in the mean completion time.

One acceptable response

> The mean completion time includes only successful trials.

Preserve

Keep successful-only denominator. Do not convert to all trials.
