# Research safeguards for revision, measurement and delivery

Use only relevant sections for the current task. R00–R50 and the existing Humanizer remain active. Language-only edits do not trigger a full study audit. Qualitative, theoretical, review and empirical work need different evidence. There is no fixed word count, sample size, metric, statistical test or numeric baseline here.

This guide adapts a user-supplied synthesis of 72 lessons. It is not a direct transcript of advisors or independent validation of a universal methodology. Private linked project records were not inspected for this upgrade. Examples are synthetic. The mapping in provenance records scope and adaptation. Venue limits and project decisions are not universal rules.

## R51 Control the task and interpret feedback on its actual target

Before changing anything, identify the reader and intended decision, authorized actions, authoritative file/ID and version, protected content, data cutoff and inclusion scope, destination requirements, deliverables, recipient, real deadline/time zone and decision owner in the existing brief. An audit does not authorize edits. Permission to edit does not authorize upload, publication or formal submission. Do not invent a deadline or repeatedly ask for authorization already granted.

Record feedback origin as author-confirmed, advisor request, peer suggestion, reviewer recommendation or assistant inference. Preserve the actual comment separately from interpretation. Use R49 to decide whether it is a general principle or a project-specific suggestion. An AI score or simulated review is not a human decision.

Anchor marks to the commented version, original page, paragraph opening and figure identifier before locating the target in the current version. Circles, crosses, arrows and underlines can target a label, wording, position or data. Crossing out a heading is not permission to delete its equation; moving the author block is not removing authors. Deduplicate duplicate photographs of a page. Record cropped images or uncertain audio instead of guessing missing instructions.

For each issue record old content, latest accepted decision, replacement versus addition, protected content, new locator, evidence and unresolved decision. A newer naming decision must not restore stale results. Keep the approved title exact across the visible first page, PDF, metadata and submission form, including punctuation, case and actual character limit. Filenames and old change reports do not prove the current displayed title.

If papers share data, compare questions, measures, findings and knowledge gains. A renamed system or title alone does not establish a distinct contribution. Record overlaps and check the selected venue's applicable disclosure policy rather than asserting a universal publication rule.

## R52 Make a concise research argument from evidence

Use the opening/concision checks in [paper guidance](paper.en.md). Abstracts and introductions should expose the actual question and reason to study it, not a technology history or a feature inventory. Check whether the opening still identifies the substantive question without brand names and sample counts. An empirical decision or an inadequate old metric is not mandatory for every theoretical paper.

Before polishing an empirical abstract, write the main supported answer with population, task, measure, numerator/denominator and comparator when applicable. Reconcile both sides with the source table. Retain reversals across conditions and meaningful exceptions. For theoretical or qualitative work use the actual argument, scope and evidence instead of forcing numbers. An incomplete study supports a working draft, not invented finished results.

A voice example supplies style, not findings. Check the actual event type, timing and preceding event before reusing a behavioral assertion. Each question must match its measured quantity; time share cannot answer how many people selected an option. Changing the question or scientific definitions requires researcher approval.

In related work explain relevant findings before the specific unresolved issue. Compare the closest work rather than a deliberately weak comparator. Distinguish a source's stated limitation from your bounded inference. The number of reviewed papers is not a citation number. Respect source-access limits and the gap checks in R48.

Give sections distinct jobs appropriate to the article type. In empirical work describe what participants actually did and saw in the procedure, define measures in methods, report observations in results and assess explanations in discussion. Removing a duplicate heading must not remove definitions, consent, exceptions or indispensable procedure. Preserve replication-critical specifications and one consistent name per construct. Cut irrelevant specifications and repetition first.

Recommendations must not turn observed preferences into demonstrated effectiveness or a universal instruction. Prospective work should specify a useful comparison, design or ordering, measures and follow-up as appropriate, explicitly as proposed rather than completed. Do not invent an implementation choice or planned study merely to make the conclusion sound substantial.

## R53 Reconcile the planned, deployed and experienced study

For empirical studies distinguish planned, recruited, started, completed and analyzed participants, sessions, tasks and events. State the independent unit for each analysis. Do not infer occupation, expertise or student status from an affiliation. Keep development checks, pilot sessions and actual sessions distinct without relabeling real data as a pilot because the sample is small.

Reconstruct the actual participant timeline and identify where measurements and instrument versions enter it. A form-submission timestamp is not necessarily the response start or the experimental event time. Both task orders being present does not establish randomized assignment or equal counterbalancing. Inspect group-by-order counts and describe possible order, fatigue or experience effects as possible explanations unless measured.

Match protocol intent to the deployed build, configuration, media version and actual run evidence, then to what participants experienced. The latest source code or demo screenshot is not evidence of an older session. Record defaults, restored screens, unlock conditions and availability. Compare translations, media length, button placement, devices and sequence before treating languages/sites as equivalent experiences. Working buttons during training do not prove equal exposure during every task. A condition required by the design may influence use; seeing it does not prove preference or planning. Separate designed difficulty from measured difficulty.

Define exclusions and retry-selection rules consistently and document when they were chosen. Distinguish incomplete, technical fault, withdrawal, retry and a truly absent event. Exclude the affected person/task/measure only with a reason. Inspect all relevant attempts before saying never. Do not replace all missing cases with zero or retroactively label a favorable selection as prespecified.

Define event semantics before counting. Separate user selection, default display, automatic restoration, visibility, repeat and timeout. Specify the analysis clock, units, start/end, pause overlap and outcome when no success event exists. Synchronize different clocks with a documented mapping before subtracting timestamps. A response window may begin at visible feedback rather than computation completion; use the onset observable in the study. Define its end, timestamp ties, no response and automatic events. No visibility timestamp means an uncertainty, not a backfilled event.

Join sources with documented person/task/attempt keys. Check duplicate keys, missing matches and join cardinality before merging; row adjacency is not identity. Match every metric to actual implementation exceptions. If logs do not record which exception occurred, state that it is unknown. Do not reconstruct nonexistent observations from a plausible rule.

## R54 Keep denominators, timing and inference honest

Distinguish people, events, opportunities, intervals and durations even when all are expressed as percentages. Choose the estimand and weighting before comparing groups. A mean of individual ratios and a pooled ratio answer different questions. In a synthetic example, ratios 1/2 and 9/10 have an individual mean of 70 percent and a pooled ratio of 10/12. The example is not study evidence or a prescribed aggregation.

Separate no opportunity from an opportunity with no action. Missing data and an undefined conditional response are not zero. Identify who or what was eligible in each window. Where one unit dominates a claim, consider a justified sensitivity check and report its scope. Do not delete that unit from the main result or select a favorable analysis after inspection. Keep planned tests separate from exploratory findings. Do not manufacture a hypothesis or p-value to upgrade a descriptive pattern.

Use verbs supported by the measurement. A displayed page does not mean it was read; in view does not mean gaze; video opened does not mean completed; reported preference does not mean selected use. Repeated failed checks may concern the same item rather than independent failures. Inspect actual scale anchors, reversal rules, item counts and raw versus weighted scoring. Conflicting behavior and questionnaire scores require source checking, not changing respondents' answers.

Name the statistic, unit and analysis count. Mean and median, range and confidence interval are not interchangeable. Distinguish percentages from percentage points and specify the base of a relative change. Use unrounded values to assess ranks. Explain rounding totals instead of distorting values to force 100 percent. Preserve exceptions without making all reported decimals longer.

Check equations with hand-computable cases appropriate to their domain, including empty/one/repeated/changed events, boundary-crossing intervals and a zero denominator. State undefined cases and units. A successful script run is insufficient. Keep event timestamp precision separate from another stream's sampling rate and from plotted bin resolution. Define equal time bins as fractions of duration, not task stages. State elapsed versus active time and split intervals at boundaries. Distinguish first-opening latency from cumulative opening duration.

Distinguish observation, reported symptom, suspected mechanism and verified cause. A slow task or failed action cannot identify a particular hardware fault. Association alone does not establish causation. No significant difference is not equivalence. Two main effects do not establish an interaction. Language/site differences alone do not identify culture as the cause. Report small-sample fractions, scope and exceptions without claiming population-wide preference.

## R55 Validate the chain across papers, figures and presentations

Use a cross-artifact trace for material findings from raw source through code, table, figure, abstract, conclusion and slides or speaking notes. Reconcile population, task, units, denominator and exceptions. Multiple copies of one calculation are not independent verification. A spreadsheet transcribed from a paper checks transcription, not reproduction. Standalone result sheets need headers, units, counts, definitions, timing and source version.

Choose citations to support the actual adjacent claim. A matching DOI verifies bibliographic identity, not support. Log access level and inspected passage. Shared terminology does not establish shared measurement; self-report, logged events and transfer versus delayed retention tests differ. Removing a reference requires checking the affected claim, comparison table, numbering, captions and actual PDF hyperlink destinations.

Label visual provenance by role: study-system capture, translated capture, conceptual illustration, component render, reconstructed demonstration, literature-reported result or reproduced result. A component render does not establish a connected backend. Cropping and annotation must retain context and use originals. Do not add player controls that imply a nonexistent device interface or substitute another project's image. Preserve rights and uncertainty.

Captions and legends identify encoding, units, count, denominator, aggregation and what each segment represents as needed. Keep legend color and order consistent with the graph. Ask whether a reader can state one supported message and one unsupported inference. This is a comprehension check, not a numerical quality score.

When moving or removing a figure, trace the claim, definitions, methods, discussion, subpanels and speaking notes that depend on it before renumbering. Do not delete unrelated tables that once shared an old figure number. Prefer the figure or table that answers the question more clearly when they duplicate information. Keep captions, notes, table heads and necessary rows readable together; position content according to the actual template.

Apply Word-first safeguards. Blank page space does not prove missing text. Compare content and column order before adjusting keep-with-next, captions or anchors. Do not invent text, shrink margins or flatten editable equations to fill space. Inspect the actual exported final artifact at reading size, including image-to-image overlap, clipped tables, subscripts, operators, indices, Thai marks and internal links. Text extraction and export success do not establish visual correctness.

Compare parallel languages by section and meaning rather than identical page breaks. Slides and speaking notes should identify the same versioned graph by name or opening statement, not just slide number. Do not invent claims absent from the displayed evidence. For decision meetings state the decision sought and rehearse with the actual presentation when access permits. A shorter talk is not authority to reverse the paper's supported account. Describe rehearsals as Not Run until performed.

## R56 Preserve responsibility, privacy and the verified deliverable

Apply the actual venue rules to authorship, anonymity, ethics, AI disclosure and funding. Exemption is not approval; consent is a separate matter. Check each site's documentation and keep necessary ethics statements when anonymizing. Do not invent a shared approval status. Distinguish research funding, registration reimbursement, expected reimbursement and approved support. Use affiliations or grant identifiers only when confirmed and suitable for that version.

Keep protected personal data out of shared artifacts. Test plausible re-identification from combinations of codes, narrow age bands, site, nationality or experience; removing names alone may be insufficient. Use least necessary disclosure and recipient-appropriate aggregation. Check hidden sheets, comments, tracked changes, ID keys, embedded images, properties, headers, footers and filenames. Local possession of raw records is not permission to share them.

Preserve the authoritative master and a recoverable checkpoint. Compare intended changes and protected areas. Do not restore an old full folder to solve one local defect. Reader-facing papers should not contain prompt residue, internal revision history or an irrelevant preview/build footer. Keep version details that support reproduction and mandatory notices. Put change explanations in the separate revision/QA record.

Before authorized upload or replacement, verify destination, recipient, permissions, file ID and current remote version. Stop on a concurrent change or uncertain authority rather than overwriting another person's work. Perform read-back after upload and compare bytes/hash when the format is preserved, or appropriate content/version checks for native conversions. A successful upload does not prove that a previously shared link reaches the inspected version. Permission to upload is not permission to submit a conference form or publish publicly.

Use Passed, Failed, Not Run and Not Applicable with reasons for operations. Keep these separate from the six scientific-support statuses in R50. Numeric reconciliation, PDF inspection, ready for advisor review and formally submitted are different claims with different evidence. A successful fallback preview does not turn a failed full-system test into an end-to-end pass. Preserve failure and partial-coverage reports. Stop delivery for identity/title/number/recipient conflicts, unresolved privacy risks or missing delivery authorization. Continue independent safe work and ask only for the necessary decision.

Record the actual file/version, checks run, checks not run, unresolved matters and decisions requested. No acceptance guarantee follows from this checklist. The five bundled scripts do not execute these human/tool inspections automatically.
