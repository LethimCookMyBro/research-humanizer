One writing contract, across the paper.

Use with ARS for the full workflow, or use the core skill on its own. Researcher decisions and required confirmation points remain in place.

  1. 01

    Understand the study

    Read the actual project, source records, code and approved results. Ask about missing or conflicting inputs.

  2. 02

    Read before drafting

    Find related papers, official venue requirements and metric definitions. Keep source locations and study evidence separate.

  3. 03

    Draft with the right voice

    Write all applicable sections in clear academic prose. Use author-approved context where available.

  4. 04

    Review the saved work

    Check claims, equations, graphs and document layout. Report what passed and what remains unverified.

Built around research constraints.

Evidence and citations

Trace quantities to approved results and raw records. Check that sources support the attached claims.

Equations and code

Define symbols and units. Map formulas to implementation and inspect mismatches.

Graphs and original images

Keep plot data and scripts. Request real study photographs and captures when the paper needs observed evidence.

Overview and infographics

Make every component and arrow explain the actual method. Do not add invented blocks for appearance.

Word and anonymous files

Preserve styles, equation objects and fields. Check final pages and identifying metadata before delivery.

Author voice

Use approved examples and accessible context. Do not invent memories, experiences or personal rationale.

Before you start

Can it draft a full research paper?

Yes, its full-paper mode guides an agent to draft every applicable section from the materials supplied. It does not turn a topic into a completed experiment or create missing results.

How is it different from a general humanizer?

It is written for research manuscripts. It keeps technical terms, scope, uncertainty, citations, mathematics and evidence constraints active while drafting and revising.

Do I need ARS?

Only for the ARS-coordinated route. The core research-paper-craft skill can also guide standalone writing, revision and read-only audits.

Does it guarantee human authorship or beat AI detectors?

No. Editing patterns are not proof of authorship. The project does not optimize for detector evasion. Follow the venue’s AI-use disclosure requirements.

Will it fix Word formatting automatically?

No. The DOCX preflight is read-only. Native Microsoft Word inspection is required before claiming that Word pagination and final layout were verified.

What has actually been tested?

The v2.3.2 release includes software and instruction-contract tests. Live ARS integration, full-paper quality and native Word repairs have not been demonstrated end to end for this adapter.

Preview, with the limits in view.

A passing checker does not certify scientific correctness or publication readiness. Use the verification record to see what was tested. The researcher remains responsible for the submitted work.
